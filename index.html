About this plan

Built for a CampusX 100-Days-of-ML learner continuing from Day 31, sleeping ~1:00 AM and waking ~7:00–7:30 AM, with a fixed 6–7 PM tuition block. The remaining ~50 CampusX topics are paced across weekdays and alternate Saturdays — at that rate the core syllabus wraps around week 9–10, leaving the rest of month 3 free for capstone projects, portfolio polishing and interview prep instead of being rushed. Every topic automatically resurfaces for 24-hour, weekly and monthly revision — you never have to plan revision by hand, just follow what today's view shows you.

Weekly structure
